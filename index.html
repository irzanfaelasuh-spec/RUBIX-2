CUBE X is a digital Rubik’s Cube experience built around precision, logic and play. Every turn you make moves real pieces through a real cube model — the same state that the timer, the solver and the final verification read from, with no shortcuts.

It treats the classic puzzle as a discipline: scramble, observe, plan, execute. Speed matters, but so does clarity — a clean view, honest timing, and a cube that always obeys the rules.

CUBE X is where technology meets the puzzle that has challenged minds for decades: a space to practise, to chase your own best time, and to explore what a cube can do.

Help & About

Turn a layer: drag across a sticker. Rotate view: drag the background (or a sticker diagonally). Zoom: wheel or pinch.
Keys: R L U D F B (Shift = counter-clockwise), Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y.
Big cubes: use Layer ± to pick the slice, Wide for Rw / 3Rw.

About AI Solve: it is not a remote AI. It reverses your recorded moves with a verified check at the end, so it solves cubes scrambled inside CUBE X (any size). CUBE X v1 · Three.js r128 (cdnjs).
